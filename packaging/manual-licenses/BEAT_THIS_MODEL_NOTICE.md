# Beat This! published model weights

The Beat This! project states that its code and published model weights are
released under the MIT License.

The same upstream license section warns that some training files are fully
copyrighted or use limited Creative Commons terms, and leaves assessment of the
impact on a particular use case to the user.

Primary reference:

- https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this#license

LayerLab downloads `small0` or `final0` on first use of the corresponding
analysis profile. The weights are not bundled in the installer/runtime archive.
