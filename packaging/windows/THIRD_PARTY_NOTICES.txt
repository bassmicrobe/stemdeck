Downloaded During First-Run Setup
---------------------------------

FFmpeg binaries are not bundled in the LayerLab test zip. They are downloaded
during first-run setup. FFmpeg builds may be distributed under LGPL or GPL
terms depending on how they are compiled. LayerLab should display the selected
FFmpeg build source and license before or during download.

Demucs model weights are not bundled in the LayerLab test zip. They are
downloaded during first use. LayerLab should display the model source and
license/usage terms before or during download.
