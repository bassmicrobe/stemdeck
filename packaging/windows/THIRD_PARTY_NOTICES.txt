Downloaded During First-Run Setup
---------------------------------

FFmpeg binaries are not bundled in the LayerLab test zip. The default gyan.dev
release essentials build is downloaded directly during first-run setup. The
provider identifies this build line as GPL. Anyone who mirrors/rebundles the
binary must provide the corresponding source and satisfy the exact build's
FFmpeg/external-library obligations.

Demucs model weights are not bundled in the LayerLab test zip. They are
downloaded during first use. They are not covered by the Demucs MIT code
license; upstream maintainers describe them as personal/research-use artifacts.
Commercial distribution/services require separate permission, replacement
weights with suitable terms, or disabling the default weight workflow.

Beat This! model weights are downloaded during first use of a High, Max, or
Ultra analysis profile and are published under the MIT License by the Beat
This! authors.
